Overview: This role primary focus will be initiating outbound phone calls to prospective customers, introducing our brand, and identifying new business opportunities. The ideal candidate is a self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced environment, possesses excellent communication skills, and remains motivated through the natural ups and downs of outbound sales.


Hourly Rate: $20 per hour


Key Responsibilities:

  • Conduct high-volume outbound calls to targeted lead lists daily.
  • Engage prospects in conversation to uncover their business needs and pain points.
  • Qualify leads based on budget, authority, and timeline to ensure a strong fit.
  • Schedule qualified sales appointments and product demonstrations for our Account Executives.
  • Overcome objections professionally using structured scripts and product training.
  • Track all activities accurately within our CRM, maintaining clean notes and follow-up tasks.
  • Meet or exceed daily and weekly metrics for call volume, connect rates, and booked meetings.

Requirements:

  • Proven experience in cold calling, telemarketing, or outbound sales (preferred but not required).
  • Exceptional verbal communication and active listening skills.
  • Experience with CEA, hydroponics, and/or agriculture a strong plus.
  • A thick skin and the ability to handle rejection with a positive attitude.
  • Strong organizational skills to manage lead lists and schedule follow-ups efficiently.
  • Basic tech literacy, including comfort using dialer systems, CRMs, and email.
  • A quiet, distraction-free workspace with high-speed internet (if working remotely).
