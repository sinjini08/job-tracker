TradingBlock is looking for an ambitious and curious Junior Software Engineer to join our team. Your Mission: What You'll Tackle

You'll be on the front lines, working with senior engineers to build and improve the technology that powers our entire trading operation. Your mission will be to learn, contribute, and grow.

Build the Future of Trading: Dive into our C#/.NET codebase, ship new features, and help us improve our core trading infrastructure.

Solve High-Speed Puzzles: Learn to tackle challenges in a high-performance environment where speed and reliability are everything. You'll help debug, design, and implement solutions.

Leverage AI-Powered Tools: Embrace the future of development by using cutting-edge AI coding assistants to accelerate your work, learn faster, and solve problems in innovative ways.

Become a Pro: Absorb everything you can! You'll see the entire development process, from the first idea to the final launch. We want you to ask questions and take on new challenges.

Innovate with a Team: Collaborate in a fast-paced environment where your ideas are valued and you're part of a team building incredible technology together.

 

Your Toolkit: What You Bring to the Table

We aren’t looking for years of experience. We're looking for passion, potential, and a solid foundation to build upon.

A Developer's Mindset: You love solving problems with code and have a good grasp of programming fundamentals from your classes, personal projects, or internships.

A Hunger to Learn: You're not afraid to say "I don't know" because you're driven to find the answer. You see every challenge as a learning opportunity.

A Collaborative Spirit: You thrive in a team, enjoy sharing ideas, and believe that the best software is built together.
